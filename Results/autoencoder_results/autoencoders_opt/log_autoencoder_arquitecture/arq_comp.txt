================================================================================
                         FINAL COMPARATIVE INSIGHTS
================================================================================

DOMINANT PATTERNS ACROSS ALL MODELS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TRAINING STRATEGY > ARCHITECTURE:
   - Pure Anomaly's gradual approach outperforms all architectural innovations
   - This suggests the learning process matters more than model design

2. LINEAR BOTTLENECK SUPERIORITY:
   - 5 of top 6 models use linear bottlenecks
   - Average 11.8% performance advantage over convolutional bottlenecks

3. OPTIMAL COMPRESSION RANGE:
   - Too little (<100x): Models memorize (Model 6)
   - Too much (>1000x): Models lose discrimination (Model 8)
   - Optimal: 600-800x (Pure Anomaly)

4. SEPARATION AS KEY METRIC:
   - ROC AUC correlates strongly with separation (r = +0.89)
   - Models focusing on separation perform better
   - Current best (0.66σ) still far from target (1.5σ)

5. PARAMETER EFFICIENCY POSSIBLE:
   - Model 5 achieves 94% of best performance with 0.23% parameters
   - Architecture optimization can dramatically reduce computational cost

FUTURE DIRECTIONS SUGGESTED BY COMPARISON:
1. Hybrid Approach: Pure Anomaly's training with Model 5's efficiency
2. Enhanced Separation: Architectures specifically designed to maximize error separation
3. Adaptive Compression: Dynamic compression based on learning progress
4. Multi-stage Training: Different strategies for different training phases
